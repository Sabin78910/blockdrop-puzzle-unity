## Full description (max 4000)
Block Drop is a colourful block puzzle that's easy to learn and hard to put down. Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board, fill a row or column to clear it, and chain clears together for huge combos.

★ SATISFYING PUZZLE PLAY
• Simple drag-and-drop controls – one finger is all you need
• Clear rows and columns at the same time for big bonus points
• Keep clearing in a row to build your STREAK multiplier
• Mega clears light up the board with sparkles, sound and haptics

★ MANY WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic – relaxing endless mode, beat your own best score
• Daily Puzzle – the same pieces for everyone, every day
• 30 Levels – reach the target score and collect up to 90 stars
• VS AI – play the same pieces as an Easy, Medium or Master AI
• 2 Players – pass and play with a friend on one phone
• Challenge – share a 6-letter code so friends play your exact pieces

★ REWARDS THAT RESPECT YOUR TIME
• Daily streak bonus that grows each day you come back
• Three new missions every day
• Level up as you play and collect 12 trophies
• Earn coins by playing well and unlock vivid colour themes: Jewel, Neon, Candy, Ocean and Sunset
• No loot boxes and no pay-to-win – coins only unlock looks

★ COMPETE ONLINE
• Global leaderboards for Classic and the Daily Puzzle
• Pick your own player name
• Works offline too – your progress is saved on your device

★ PLAY YOUR WAY
• No timers – think as long as you like
• Hints when you're stuck
• A friendly break reminder after long sessions
• Sound and vibration can be turned off any time

Download Block Drop and see how big a combo you can build!
