Block Drop Puzzle is a relaxing 8×8 block game with a satisfying combo system.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag one of three pieces onto the board
• Fill a full row or column to clear it
• Clear several lines in a row to build a combo multiplier
• The game ends when none of your pieces fit

FEATURES
• Classic endless mode
• Daily challenge: everyone gets the same pieces each day
• Best score saved on your device
• Plays offline, no account needed
